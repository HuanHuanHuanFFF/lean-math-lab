# External check — not a new positive dependency

Primary source checked: Bugeaud–Evertse–Győry, *S-parts of values of univariate polynomials, binary forms and decomposable forms at integral points*, author PDF, Theorems 2.1–2.2 (printed page 4).

Source: https://irma.math.unistra.fr/~bugeaud/travaux/BuEvGy-AAdef.pdf

The text and the page image were both read. The strong exponent 1/degree+epsilon comes with an **ineffective** constant. The effective alternative has exponent 1−kappa and a different, weaker quantitative input. No numerical kappa adequate for this task was established here, and neither theorem is an input to the new positive results.

Our specialization: for f(X)=X(X−1)…(X−8) and S={2,3,5,7}, epsilon=1/90 yields an ineffective S-part upper bound C n^(11/10). The frozen i9 scalar lower bound implies S-part at least n^(27/10)/R^(1/10). This gives finiteness with an unspecified C, **not an executable global height**. The distinction is precisely why this was not registered as effective completion. This observation is not claimed as a novelty.
