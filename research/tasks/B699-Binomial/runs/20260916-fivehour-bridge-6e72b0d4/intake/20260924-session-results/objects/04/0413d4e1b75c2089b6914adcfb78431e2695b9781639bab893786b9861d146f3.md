# MISSING / recovery limitations

## Research artifact recovery

All 14 research evidence ZIPs referenced in the visible conversation are present and included exactly. Their sidecar SHA-256/check files that still exist in the working attachment area are also included. The earlier 9-round master archive is included exactly as a historical artifact. No whole research round listed in `ROUND_INDEX.md` is missing.

## Exact chat transcript limitation

The available tools do **not** expose a lossless raw export of the ChatGPT conversation UI or the original serialized tool-result cards. Therefore this archive does **not** claim to contain a byte-for-byte chat export.

`conversation/VISIBLE_CONVERSATION_RECONSTRUCTED.md` preserves every user research/archive prompt that is present in the currently loaded conversation context and maps each research turn to the exact delivered evidence package/core files. Assistant final-delivery prose is represented by the exact delivered REPORT/PROOFS/HANDOFF bytes and round metadata, not falsely labeled as a verbatim UI export. Intermediate commentary/update text and raw tool wrapper JSON may be incomplete.

The final assistant reply announcing this ZIP cannot be embedded byte-for-byte into the ZIP it hashes without a self-reference cycle; the current user request is included, and the final reply remains in the chat UI.

## Temporary/process files

Files never attached/delivered and never included in an evidence ZIP (temporary scratch files, unsaved stdout, abandoned working directories, hidden reasoning) cannot be recovered and are not fabricated. Hidden chain-of-thought is intentionally excluded.

## Deduplication

Extracted evidence-package members are deduplicated **only when their SHA-256 bytes are identical**. Every logical source path is retained in `DEDUP_SOURCE_MAP.tsv` and `rounds/*/MEMBERS.tsv`. Original evidence ZIPs themselves are never modified or deduplicated internally.

## Mathematical scope

Archive/CRC/SHA verification is not a mathematical proof. No new Lean run, repository modification, external independent review, complete i=3 proof, or complete B699 closure is claimed by this archive build.
