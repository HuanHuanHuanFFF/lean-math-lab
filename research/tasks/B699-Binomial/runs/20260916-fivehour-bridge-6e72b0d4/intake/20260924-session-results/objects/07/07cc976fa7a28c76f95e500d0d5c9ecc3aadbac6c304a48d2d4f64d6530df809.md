# EVIDENCE LEVEL

This package contains:

- author-level paper mathematics;
- deterministic complete finite searches for the stated q=3,4,5 jet systems;
- two-prime modular full-rank checks;
- exact rational recovery and factorization of every modular low-rank q3/q4 endpoint;
- two exact Bellman implementations using different signature sets/orders;
- clean-output byte comparison against frozen certificates.

It does **not** contain:

- Lean kernel verification;
- external independent full-chain mathematical review;
- actual recovery of the fixed polynomial G or its irreducible factors;
- a complete proof of i=9 or Erdős Problem 699.
