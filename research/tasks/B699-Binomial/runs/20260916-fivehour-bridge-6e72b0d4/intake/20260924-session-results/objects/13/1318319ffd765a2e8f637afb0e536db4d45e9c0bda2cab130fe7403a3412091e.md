# PROOFS — ODD-SAT3–5 and EDGE8 for the same fixed G

## 0. Frozen setting and notation

We use the frozen K152 construction for `i=9`.  A legal original input has

```text
10 <= j <= floor(n/2),  k=n-j,  J=jk,  400 | n,
```

and `NC9(n,j)` means that no prime `p>=11` divides both `C(n,9)` and `C(n,j)`.
Since 9 is composite, this is the same prime threshold as the original `p>=9` requirement.
All complete prime powers, the same original `(n,j)`, and both value branches of the frozen construction remain adopted.

The construction supplies one fixed nonzero

```text
G in Z[N,X],  deg_w G <=305,  deg_X G <=152,
wt(N)=1, wt(X)=2.
```

Every legal large NC9 input in the zero-value branch gives `G(n,J)=0`, and `G(n,X)` is not identically zero on that row.
A *loadable factor* is a primitive `Q[N,X]`-irreducible factor of this same `G` that vanishes at some legal NC9 point.  Different factors may load different points, but all divide the same `G`.

For a nonvertical irreducible factor `H`, write

```text
D=deg_w H, e=deg_X H, epsilon=D-2e >=0.
```

At row `r=3,...,8`, let `m_(r,s)` be the ordinary order at the real source point
`(r,s(r-s))`, and set

```text
mu_r = sum_s m_(r,s),  Delta_r=e-mu_r >=0.
```

At the three diagonal points use local coordinates

```text
u=N-r, t=X-s^2-su,  r=2s in {4,6,8},
```

ordinary order `m`, weighted `(1,2)` order `w`, and

```text
kappa_r=2m-w >=0.
```

The source capacity charged by one factor in row `r` is `2 Delta_r+kappa_r` on even rows and `2 Delta_r` on odd rows.

Strip all powers `(N-r)^{v_r}` from the fixed `G`, let `h=deg_X G`, `V=sum v_r`, and define

```text
E=305-2h-V >=0.
```

The frozen source-budget proof gives 2,035 possible vertical states.  The previous round proved

```text
|C_G| <=8.
```

This round studies equality.

## 1. Soundness of the complete relaxed eight-factor ledger

### 1.1 Balanced factor signatures

For factors with `epsilon=0`, we adopt the complete weak signature partition already used in the COVER8 proof:

- ordinary total defect at least 4;
- total defect 3, with the frozen low-degree/early-row or diagonal-kappa alternative;
- total defect 2, including GAMMA5/TAIL567 restrictions;
- single defect in the frozen near-saturated ranges;
- saturated `25<=e<=31`, paying two units of diagonal kappa;
- saturated `32<=e<=42`, paying one unit of diagonal kappa;
- saturated `e>=43`, provisionally free;
- all actual B22/S/L special signatures.

The ledger keeps only degree and six source-capacity coordinates.  It deliberately forgets known extra roots and TRACE constraints, hence enlarges the true feasible set.

There are 467 raw named signatures, 460 distinct degree/cost pairs and 116 Pareto-minimal pairs.

### 1.2 Positive factors are modeled optimistically

Suppose an actual set of eight loadable factors contains `A` factors with positive `epsilon`.
Then `A<=E`, every such factor has `e>=3`, and every source charge is nonnegative.
Replace each by a virtual item

```text
e=3, source charge=(0,0,0,0,0,0), epsilon charge=1.
```

This replacement cannot increase the necessary degree or source cost.  Unused excess `E-A` may belong to unselected factors of `Gbar` and is simply discarded.  Therefore every actual eight-factor configuration maps into this relaxed ledger.

### 1.3 Two independent exact Bellman computations

Both implementations regenerate all 2,035 vertical states from the frozen source lower orders.

- Implementation A uses the 116 Pareto signatures and memoized recursive Bellman.
- Implementation B uses all 460 distinct raw signatures in reverse order and the same exact integer recurrence, without Pareto deletion.

For `n` balanced items and capacity `c`, both evaluate

```text
M(0,c)=0,
M(n,c)=min_s (e_s+M(n-1,c-cost_s)).
```

They return identical survivor indices and identical minima for every state.
The necessary frontier is exactly

```text
454 E=0 states,
2 E=1 states,
0 states with E>=2.
```

The two positive states are indices 1980 and 1999 recorded in `frontier_key_states.json`.

## 2. Reduction of positive E to odd saturated e=3,4,5

Assume eight loadable factors and `E>0`.  By the complete ledger, only state 1980 or 1999 is possible, and in both `E=1`.
The sum of all nonnegative excesses over every factor of `Gbar` is at most `E`; hence the selected positive factor has

```text
epsilon=1, D=2e+1,
```

and all other factors have zero excess.

If this factor has zero source charge, then necessarily

```text
Delta_3=...=Delta_8=0,
kappa_4=kappa_6=kappa_8=0.
```

For state 1980, seven balanced factors need degree at least 140, so `e<=145-140=5`.
For state 1999, they need at least 144, so `e<=147-144=3`.
Together with frozen `e>=3`, only `e=3,4,5` occur.

It remains to prove ODD-SAT3–5.

## 3. Common finite classification for odd saturated factors

Fix `q=e in {3,4,5}` and suppose

```text
D=2q+1, mu_r=q for all six rows, z>=14, kappa_4=kappa_6=kappa_8=0.
```

### 3.1 Finite multiplicity data

The monomial space

```text
B_(2q+1)={N^a X^b : a+2b<=2q+1, 0<=b<=q}
```

has dimension

```text
K=(q+1)(q+2),
```

namely 20, 30, 42 for `q=3,4,5`.

If the coefficient of `X^q` vanishes at a source row `r`, then `H(r,X)` has degree at most `q-1` yet the prescribed ordinary orders sum to `q`; hence `H(r,X)=0` and `N-r` divides `H`.  A loadable irreducible factor of X-degree `q` cannot be such a vertical factor.  Thus every row has degree `q` and the ordinary source orders exhaust all roots of `H(r,X)`.

Consequently each row is represented by a nonnegative composition of `q` among its actual source values.  The row option counts are:

```text
q=3: 4,10,10,20,20,35
q=4: 5,15,15,35,35,70
q=5: 6,21,21,56,56,126.
```

### 3.2 Exact ordinary and weighted jets

At a non-diagonal source, ordinary order `m` imposes all Hasse coefficients with `i+j<m`.
At a diagonal source, `kappa=0` means weighted order `2m`, so all local coefficients with

```text
i+2j<2m
```

vanish after the exact shear `X=s^2+su+t`.

For a monomial `N^aX^b`, the coefficient of `u^i t^j` is computed by the integer formula

```text
C(b,j) sum_z C(b-j,z) (s^2)^(b-j-z) s^z C(a,i-z) r^(a-i+z).
```

No coefficient-height or denominator box is imposed.

### 3.3 Source-line pruning

For `t=0,...,8`, let

```text
ell_t=X-t(N-t).
```

The restriction `H(N,tN-t^2)` has degree at most `D=2q+1` in `N`.
At a non-diagonal source it has order at least `m`; at the diagonal source on that line, `kappa=0` raises the guaranteed line order to `2m`.
If the sum over distinct row coordinates exceeds `D`, then `ell_t|H`, contradicting irreducibility.
Equality is retained.

### 3.4 Modular full rank and exact low-rank reception

All jet systems are homogeneous integer systems in the K monomial coefficients.
Rank K modulo a prime proves that a K-by-K integer minor is nonzero, hence the characteristic-zero system has only the zero solution.
No affine “no point modulo p” inference is used.

Every modular low-rank endpoint for q=3,4 is rebuilt over `ZZ`/`QQ` using SymPy `DomainMatrix`.  Nullspaces are made primitive and factored exactly.
The entire modular enumeration is repeated at primes 32749 and 32719; the residual files are byte-identical.

## 4. q=3 exact endpoint

The two modular runs each leave 16 configurations.
The exact receiver gives:

```text
4 rational full-rank systems,
11 one-dimensional spaces with reducible unique polynomial,
1 one-dimensional fixed exception.
```

The eleven reducible forms include products of source lines such as

```text
X (N-r) (N-X-1) (2N-X-4)
```

and one analogous factor with `3N-X-9`.

The remaining primitive fixed polynomial is

```text
4N^7 -144N^6 +3N^5X +2176N^5 -72N^4X -17880N^4
-3N^3X^2 +687N^3X +86236N^3 +45N^2X^2 -3240N^2X
-244056N^2 -249NX^2 +7626NX +375264N
+9X^3 +405X^2 -7164X -241920.
```

It has

```text
weighted degree 7,
degree_X 3,
primitive l1 norm 987183 < 2^20 < 2^24980.
```

The frozen small-coefficient consumer says that any nonzero integer polynomial of weighted degree at most 11 and `l1<=2^24980` vanishing at a legal `400|n` NC9 point already yields Common9.  Therefore this fixed polynomial is not loadable.  No irreducibility claim for it is needed.

## 5. q=4 exact endpoint

Each prime leaves the same 17 configurations.  Over `Q`, all have rank 29 and a one-dimensional kernel.  The unique primitive polynomial in every case factors nontrivially.
Examples include

```text
(N-6)(3N-X-9)(N^2-9N-X+20)^3,
-X(N-8)(N-X-1)(2N-X-4)(3N-X-9),
```

and the complete list is stored in `q4_exact.json`.
Thus no `Q[N,X]`-irreducible factor remains.

## 6. q=5 leading coefficient and root-sum gate

For speed, q=5 uses one additional necessary condition before the full jet rank.
Write

```text
H=A(N)X^5+B(N)X^4+...,
deg A<=1, deg B<=3.
```

Let `S_r` be the sum of the five row roots with multiplicity.  Saturation gives

```text
A(r)S_r=-B(r).
```

Hence `U_r=A(r)S_r` is the restriction of a polynomial of degree at most 3 to `r=3,...,8`; its two fourth differences vanish.
Putting `A(N)=aN+b` gives a homogeneous 2-by-2 linear system in `(a,b)`.
The checker symbolically verifies the determinant formula used to predict the row-8 root sum.
A nonzero null vector must also satisfy `A(r)!=0` for all six rows; otherwise `N-r|H` as in §3.1.

The corrected complete run at both primes gives identical counts:

```text
nodes 75706, final row-8 options 19447,
line rejects 1051900, z rejects 10,
root-sum rejects 35215, leading-coefficient rejects 3058,
full-rank rejects 824561, residual 0.
```

Thus q=5 has no characteristic-zero candidate.

## 7. ODD-SAT3–5 and the source-cost conclusion

Sections 4–6 prove:

> If a loadable irreducible factor has `D=2e+1`, `e in {3,4,5}`, all six `Delta_r=0`, `z>=14`, and all three diagonal `kappa` equal zero, then a contradiction follows.

All source costs are nonnegative integers.  Therefore every such loadable positive factor consumes at least one unit in some one of the six source-capacity coordinates.

## 8. Closing the positive-E frontier

The independent payoff checker retains the positive factor at the most favorable degree `e=3` and subtracts exactly one unit from each feasible coordinate in turn.

For state 1980 (`h=145`, capacity `(0,1,6,1,0,6)`), the best totals are

```text
row4 151, row5 167, row6 155, row8 151.
```

For state 1999 (`h=147`, capacity `(0,0,4,5,4,1)`), they are

```text
row5 175, row6 157, row7 164, row8 157.
```

Every value exceeds h.  Hence neither positive state can contain eight actual loadable factors.
Since the complete relaxed ledger had no other positive-E survivor, equality at eight forces `E=0`.

When `E=0`, the nonnegative excesses of all nonconstant factors of `Gbar` sum to zero, so each equals zero.  Also `2h+V=305`, yielding the EDGE8 conclusions.

## 9. Scope relative to the original problem

This proof is entirely at the fixed-G factor layer.  It does not identify the coefficients of G or its factors and does not eliminate the remaining 454 E0 necessary states.
It preserves the same original `(n,j)`, complete prime powers, `H|G`, and the distinction between necessary source data and actual NC9 points.
No finite-field vector, resource state, or small polynomial is promoted to an original-problem counterexample.
