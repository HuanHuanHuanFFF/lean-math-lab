## 2. 外部出版输入

M. A. Bennett, M. Filaseta, O. Trifonov,
*On the factorization of consecutive integers*, PDF dated February 26, 2007,
Theorem 2.1, printed page 4.

Official author-hosted PDF checked in this round:
https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/BFTpaper0207.pdf

Adopted input only: for the specified prime pairs, a small difference between
prime-power multiples forces a cofactor lower bound, except for the theorem's
small-input case and finite exception table. The three parameters used here are
0.285, 0.258, and 0.216. Both multiples exceed 1000; differences are 2 or 5.
The theorem allows non-coprime cofactors and zero exponents. It is not proved
by this package's replay program.

本轮通过网页工具查看了该页图像和相关文字；没有把整篇论文复制进证据包。
