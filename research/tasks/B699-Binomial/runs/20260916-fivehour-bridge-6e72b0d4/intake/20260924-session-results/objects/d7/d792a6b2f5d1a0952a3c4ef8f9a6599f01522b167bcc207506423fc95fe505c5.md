# MISSING / recovery limitations

This archive is intentionally conservative. It does **not** claim materials that the current chat/file tools cannot retrieve exactly.

## Conversation transcript limitations

- There is no platform-native tool available here that exports the entire ChatGPT UI conversation as raw bytes/JSON.
- `TRANSCRIPT/USER_MESSAGES.md` is a best-effort transcription from the visible current conversation context, not a signed/raw platform export.
- `TRANSCRIPT/ASSISTANT_FINAL_DELIVERIES.md` maps every research round to exact generated report/proof/evidence artifacts, but does **not** claim that the assistant-final prose is byte-identical to the UI message.
- Streaming assistant commentary and exploratory tool-result messages that were never saved to files cannot be recovered byte-for-byte. Recoverable logs/validation files/console excerpts are included through the content store and source map.
- Hidden chain-of-thought/private reasoning is excluded, as requested.

## Temporary/in-memory research state

- Temporary Python/Jupyter state, shell state, and generator scripts that were never exported as attachments or ZIP members may be unavailable. The earlier full archive already documented examples such as temporary C14/C15 generator files.
- External webpages/PDFs viewed during research are not mirrored here unless a snapshot was explicitly embedded in a session-generated evidence package. URLs/citations in reports remain the record of those external sources.
- The git repository itself is not copied or modified by this archival pass. Embedded source snapshots inside session-produced evidence ZIPs are preserved because they are bytes of the session artifacts.

## Current archive self-reference

The outer ZIP produced by this archival turn is the **current complete archive**. It cannot recursively contain a copy of itself. The previous mid-session full archive (`C-B699-ALL-SESSION-OUTPUTS-2026-09-24.zip`) is preserved verbatim as a historical artifact.

## Evidence-level warning

Byte integrity, ZIP CRC, SHA-256 checks, replay logs, deterministic finite certificates, and same-author dual implementations are not Lean proofs or external independent mathematical review. No complete B699 closure is claimed.
