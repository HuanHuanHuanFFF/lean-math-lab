# CONTENTS

- `REPORT.md` — result summary and exact frontier change.
- `PROOFS.md` — mathematical reduction and completeness proof.
- `FAILURES.md` — boundaries and non-claims.
- `HANDOFF.md` — next research entry point.
- `FRONTIER.json` — machine-readable status.
- `REPLAY.md`, `replay.sh`, `full_replay.sh` — quick and full replay.
- `evidence/` — owned source code.
- `receipts/` / `outputs/` — campaign and replay logs.
- `certificates/` — aggregate and cross-check JSON.
- `sources/` — frozen source excerpts and prior-round handoffs.
- `SHA256SUMS`, `MANIFEST.tsv`, `PACKAGE_AUDIT.json` — integrity metadata.
