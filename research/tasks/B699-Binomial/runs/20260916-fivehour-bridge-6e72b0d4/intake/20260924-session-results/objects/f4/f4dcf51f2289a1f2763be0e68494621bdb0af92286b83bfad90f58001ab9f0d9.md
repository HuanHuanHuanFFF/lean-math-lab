# Exploration is not extra accepted mathematics

four_norm_product is the successful pre-package generator run. Its certificate is independently checked under certificates/.
four_original and capped runs are unsuccessful search diagnostics; exit124 is timeout, exit1 means no target found by that run.
Mixed runs have NO accepted characteristic-zero certificate. They remain OPEN regardless of necessary equations, partial bases or former modular signals.
.in files use whitespace: variable count, input count, target variable; weights; variable names; for each polynomial: term count, then numerator denominator exponents.
They are diagnostics and can be passed to src/gbtrace.cpp compiled against GMP. Input .in alone is not the accepted original-equation reconstruction.
The final accepted generator reconstructs from equations in src/derive.py; the verifier independently reconstructs in src/check_standard.py.
No exhaustive claim is made about every temporary exploration file.
