# REPLAY

## Environment

Python3.13.5, SymPy1.14.0 in the generation environment. No network or repository is needed for replay.
External theorem reading is a separate source-adoption step, not a runtime operation.

## Complete replay

```sh
bash replay.sh
```

The script checks every internal SHA-256, creates a temporary directory, regenerates exact.json,
runs the standard-library implementation without importing SymPy, and compares both regenerated JSON files
byte for byte with the frozen certificates. Frozen certificates and inputs are never overwritten.

The archive-check receipt is written outside the finished ZIP after extracting and replaying that actual ZIP.
A successful receipt checks integrity and executable finite computations; it is not Lean or a machine proof
of the adopted theorem, geometric applicability argument, or the original unbounded B699 proposition.

## Scope of computations

- Five complete sparse three-variable identities in the second implementation.
- Exact Weierstrass discriminant and original-recovery formulas.
- 64 finite multiplicity profiles and 64 infinity degree profiles (regression).
- 10 exact polynomial root-count profiles; not claimed to possess sections.
- 2713 partitions of m=1,...,20; finite multiplicity inequality regression.
- Three large integer-row applications: only sufficient-consumer hypotheses, no enumeration of j.
- Five deliberately corrupted certificate variants rejected.
- m2 degree classes enumerated only over the whole interval supplied by the proved degree bound.
