# Missing / recovery boundaries

## Research artifacts
No research round from R01–R12 is missing its original evidence ZIP in the active runtime. All 12 ZIPs pass `zipfile.testzip()`.

The earlier interim aggregate archive (`A-i9-ALL-SESSION-OUTPUTS-2026-09-24.zip`) is also recoverable and preserved.

## Conversation transcript
The runtime does **not** expose a raw export of the current ChatGPT conversation or its UI tool-event stream. Therefore:
- exact per-message timestamps are unavailable;
- the byte-exact assistant final-message text is not guaranteed recoverable as a raw transcript artifact;
- raw visible tool-result payloads are not guaranteed recoverable as a UI export.

The `conversation/` files are explicitly reconstructed from the visible conversation context available to this archive turn. They must not be treated as a forensic UI export.

## Temporary / discarded research scratch
Some exploratory temporary files, abandoned partial outputs and superseded failed runs were never delivered as attachments and may not be recoverable. They are not fabricated. Where a final evidence ZIP records a correction or failed boundary, that record is preserved.

## Upstream material from other conversations
This archive intentionally does not import unrelated other-conversation evidence. If a round evidence ZIP itself contains an adopted upstream dependency/source copy, that internal member is preserved because it was part of this session's delivered artifact.

## Current-turn final UI response
The final assistant response that links this ZIP is emitted after the ZIP is created. A prepared delivery summary is stored, but the exact post-build UI message cannot be inside its own already-hashed ZIP.
