## 1. 新的同源 POWER 恒等式

```math

\quad 2nu=hd-Q,
\quad vnu^2=PQ^2-1,
```


```math
\boxed{

  \left(c d^3 2^{s-1}-L\right).}
```

```math
\boxed{v_2(R)=s+1+v_2(v).}
```

```math
\boxed{s=v_2(R)-2.}
```
