# Evidence levels

## Highest level actually claimed in this bundle

- Author paper proof / symbolic derivation in the round `PROOFS.md` files.
- Same-session deterministic program replay, exact integer checks, coefficient identities, bounded implementation regressions, hash verification, and ZIP integrity tests where recorded.
- Historical frozen inputs are adopted only at the evidence grade stated in each round's `SOURCE_ADOPTION.md` / source snapshot.

## Explicitly not claimed

- No new Lean proof or Lean full-chain verification in C–C6.
- No external independent mathematical review of the new C–C6 arguments.
- No complete proof of B699.
- No completion of the global residual index set R7.
- Finite scans/replays are not promoted to unrestricted theorems unless a symbolic proof in PROOFS supplies the infinite argument.
- Weak models and local compatibility families in FAILURES are not NC6 or B699 counterexamples unless explicitly proved to satisfy the complete original conditions (none in this bundle are so claimed).

Repository status while packaging: read-only; no modification, commit, push, PR, merge, or publication.
