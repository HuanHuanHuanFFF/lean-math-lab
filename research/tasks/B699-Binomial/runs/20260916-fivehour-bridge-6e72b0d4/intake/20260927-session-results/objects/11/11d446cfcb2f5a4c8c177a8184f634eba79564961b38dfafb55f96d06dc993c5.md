## 1. EARLY-NEAR19–20

Let H be Q[N,X]-irreducible, balanced, monic in X after rational scaling, `D(H)=2q`, `deg_X H=q`, q in {19,20}. Assume z>=14 and exactly one ordinary deficiency: either Δ4=1 or Δ5=1, with the other five rows saturated. Suppose for contradiction κ4=κ6=κ8=0.

Write

`H=X^q-S(N)X^(q-1)+T(N)X^(q-2)+...`, `deg S<=2`, `deg T<=4`.

Rows6,7,8 are saturated, so their root sums are integers. Three consecutive integer values of a rational quadratic imply integer values at every integer N through the Newton form

`S(6+t)=S6+t(S7-S6)+binom(t,2)(S8-2S7+S6)`.

Thus the unique residual root on the deficient row is an integer. The finite generator enumerates every nonnegative source-multiplicity vector with row sum q (or q-1 on the deficient row), reconstructs this unique residual root, and checks:
- all six common root-sum values from one quadratic S;
- all six second elementary symmetric sums from one quartic T;
- z>=14;
- no impossible residual-root collision at a source with ordinary multiplicity0;
- all nine source-line loads <=2q under κ=0;
- the strengthened weighted-plane genus inequality used in the already adopted EARLY-NEAR16–18 proof.

Absolute irreducibility needed for that genus inequality follows exactly as before: if there are d geometric conjugate factors then d|q and d divides all rational source multiplicities, hence d|(6q-1); gcd(q,6q-1)=1, so d=1.

The complete root counts are 308,245,493,386 for (q,row)=(19,4),(19,5),(20,4),(20,5). A second complete enumeration uses a different saturated anchor row and produces the identical sets.

Fixing the six row polynomials gives `H=H0+WQ`, `W=prod_{r=3}^8(N-r)`, `D(Q)<=2q-6`, `dim Q=(q-2)^2`. The full ordinary and weighted Hasse jets for κ=0 give an augmented homogeneous matrix with `(q-2)^2+1` columns. Every one of the 1,432 matrices has full column rank mod32749 and mod32719. The chosen mod32719 minors are separately reconstructed by local polynomial convolution from the stored jet row IDs, and every determinant is nonzero. Since the Lagrange denominators divide120 and neither prime divides120, these nonzero modular integer minors imply nonzero characteristic-zero minors.

Contradiction. Therefore the actual factor must pay at least one diagonal κ.

## 2. q=8, Δ7=2

`R(X)=X^2-A X+P`,

`-T3+5T4-10T5+10T6-5T7+T8=0`

Thus κ4+κ6+κ8>=1.

## 4. Exact price certificates for 13 states

For a fixed state capacity C, suppose every individually admissible signature `(e,c)` satisfies

`K <= e + sum_j w_j c_j`, with all w_j>=0.

Any eight-factor realization then satisfies

`sum e_i >= 8K - sum_j w_j C_j`.

`certificates/fees/price_certificates.json` audits every admissible signature exactly with rational arithmetic for13 states. The resulting integer lower bounds are strictly greater than the available h, so all13 states are impossible.
