# Discovery, not the formal replay

These small logs and exploratory scripts record the cost diagnostics that selected the new geometry. They do not replace proof or certificate acceptance. In particular, “newT11” applied to every state in a discovery probe is a counterfactual only; the formal replay restricts T11 to 1699 and 1701 and preserves the other states' witnesses.

The direct tool invocation interrupted by a timeout did not complete or emit a PASS receipt. Its partial console is preserved. All accepted certificates were regenerated by the later completed author run.

The source code may contain original session-local paths. Use ../code/replay.py for the complete portable offline reproduction.
