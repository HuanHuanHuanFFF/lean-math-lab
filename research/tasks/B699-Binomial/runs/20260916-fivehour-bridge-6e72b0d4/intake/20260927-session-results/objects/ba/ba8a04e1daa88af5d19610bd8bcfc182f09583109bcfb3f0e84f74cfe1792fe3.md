# Replay contract and integrity

The formal driver is `code/replay.py`. It must be run to a fresh output directory. It verifies all adopted input hashes, compiles local C++ sources, and performs this round's complete new computations only.

The 125 deterministic certificate files are compared as a complete filename-to-SHA-256 map by `code/compare_replay.py`. A missing or extra certificate is an error, not a partial PASS. Runtime-dependent environment and timings are stored separately.

A complete replay regenerates 11 new geometric profiles, receives 514 modular nonzero minors, resolves the three exact rational kernels, computes 12 full integer determinants (including all five q10 main cases), rebuilds 9 same-state quotient systems with 12 generator/receiver executions, and compares both full raw-capacity/exact-used DP tables. No unspecified affine lifting or coefficient-box assumption is made.

The optional but bundled next1701 probe is checked separately against `discovery/next1701_late_cost_diagnostic.json`; its result is only the stated conditional budget benefit. No q10 late-cost exclusion is claimed.

Author-run receipts establish the initial certificate set. Clean-extraction receipts establish an offline replay from archive bytes. No receipt is included in its own digest. The top-level manifest excludes only itself; separate final-ZIP receipts contain the final archive digest. Hash correctness or replay agreement is not external independent mathematical review and does not prove frozen historical dependencies.
